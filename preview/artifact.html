    How to play

    A or Z / Enter
    Pick a fighter, start a battle, attack
    B or X
    Use the selected item in battle, or go back
    Arrows
    Browse your roster, or choose an item in battle

    Types: STORM beats SWAMP, SWAMP beats FLAME, FLAME beats STORM. A strong matchup hits for 1.5×.

    Items: about one barcode in nine is an item instead of a fighter: POTION heals, POWER raises attack, GUARD raises defense. Your bag holds five.

    Rare codes: real product barcodes (with a valid check digit) get +10% stats. Codes with four repeated digits in a row, or that read the same backwards, get +30%.
