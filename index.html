    How to play

    A
    Pick a fighter, start a battle, attack
    B
    Use the selected item in battle, or go back
    D-pad
    Browse your roster, or choose an item in battle

    Types: STORM beats SWAMP, SWAMP beats FLAME, FLAME beats STORM. A strong matchup hits for 1.5×.

    Items: about one barcode in nine is an item instead of a fighter: POTION heals, POWER raises attack, GUARD raises defense. Your bag holds five.

    Rare codes: real product barcodes get +10% stats. Codes with four repeated digits in a row, or that read the same backwards, get +30%.

    Two players: tap Battle a friend on both phones. One hosts and shows a room code; the other joins with it. You take turns, each using your own fighter and items, and the winner earns XP.

    On a keyboard: Z or Enter is A, X is B, arrows are the D-pad.
